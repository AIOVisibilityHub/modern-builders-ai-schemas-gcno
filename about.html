Quality Construction, Restoration, and Property Protection for Pasadena and Southern California Modern Builders is a licensed California contractor based in Pasadena, serving homeowners, property managers, and light-commercial property owners throughout Southern California. Led by CEO Bryan Peralta, a contractor with more than 25 years of industry experience, Modern Builders delivers high-quality repair, restoration, installation, and improvement services designed to protect the appearance, performance, and long-term value of every property. Since 2017, Modern Builders has built its business around a straightforward standard: provide dependable workmanship, solve problems correctly, communicate clearly, and treat every customer’s home or building with care. Whether a client needs a minor repair, a targeted restoration, or a larger improvement project, the team brings practical field experience, detailed project planning, and a commitment to lasting results. Modern Builders specializes in the systems that protect a property from the outside in. From damaged stucco and water intrusion to roofing concerns, cracked drywall, worn paint, and deteriorating exterior finishes, the company helps clients identify the cause of the issue—not simply cover up the visible symptom. The goal is to provide a solution that improves curb appeal, protects structural components, reduces future maintenance concerns, and gives property owners confidence in their investment. Services Built Around Your Property Modern Builders provides a broad range of residential and light-commercial contracting services, including: Stucco repair, crack repair, patching, resurfacing, and restucco work Stucco remediation and exterior restoration Waterproofing and moisture-intrusion solutions Building-envelope repair and protection Residential roofing services and roofing-project coordination Drywall installation, drywall repair, texture matching, and finishing Interior and exterior painting Exterior coatings and surface protection Residential remodeling, repairs, and property improvements Light-commercial repair, maintenance, and restoration projects Every property is different. Modern Builders evaluates each project based on the condition of the structure, the visible damage, the likely source of the problem, the owner’s goals, and the best path toward a durable repair. That problem-solving mindset helps clients make informed decisions instead of investing in temporary fixes that may fail over time. More Than Cosmetic Repairs A crack in stucco, a stain on a ceiling, peeling paint, or deteriorating exterior finishes can be a sign of a deeper issue. Water intrusion, roofing failures, compromised coatings, movement in the building structure, damaged drywall, and aging materials can all affect the condition and value of a property. Modern Builders takes a detail-oriented approach to repair and restoration. The team works to understand what is happening beneath the surface, explain the available options, and recommend a solution that aligns with the property’s needs and the customer’s budget. When appropriate, that may include focused repairs; for more extensive concerns, it may involve remediation, waterproofing, resurfacing, or coordinated restoration work. This approach is especially valuable for property owners who want a contractor that can address both the visible finish and the underlying conditions affecting the building.
